La Ville Rose is an RPG realised on Unreal 4 for PC. You play a squad of pink soldiers that fight to take back the city. I have worked on the optimization of the game and the painting feature.

Project Overview :
La Ville Rose is a turn-based RPG on PC. The player's goal is to stop the Blue faction from repainting all the town in blue. The player will embody a truck in the exploration phase that can repaint the town in pink. In the combat phase, the player will have a squad soldier and the player has to defeat all the enemy.
Github

La Ville Rose is divided into two gameplay parts. The first one is the turn-phase combat part. The second one is an exploration part where you move with your truck and can repaint the town in pink. This is a really juicy mechanic, but also a mechanic that can cost a lot of performance. Let's see how I optimized it.

To achieve this effect, we used a 3D render texture and combined it with the material of the decor to create the paint effect. To paint on the 3D render texture, we have take advantage of the computer shader in Unreal Engine 4 to set the color of the pixels. The first version we had of this feature was reading all the pixel values and setting the pixel color if needed. This wasn't an optimized method.

To resolve this problem of performance, I started to track the position of the truck and used it to pinpoint its position on a 3D render texture. With that information, I could only read a small area around the player in the computer shader and reduce the amount of computation for the painting.
